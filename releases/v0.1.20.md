San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.20: crash and drawing fixes in the renderer and runtime.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.20-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.20-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** fixes found while porting other games, in the renderer (RT64) and the runtime the game runs on; none was reported in this game. With **Framerate** above the game's own (the frames drawn in between), a rare crash as the window changes size is gone, models close to the camera and a band at the bottom of the screen no longer drop out of the in-between frames, and a texture that moves in one step no longer slides there. Sprites drawn at a fixed depth sit at that depth instead of a step behind it. A division by zero in the game's code gives the console's result instead of crashing, and a thread queued twice no longer hangs the game. Nothing to set: settings and saves are as before.

The [README](https://github.com/amazingalek/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
