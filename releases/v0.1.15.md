San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.15: menu labels and race text show again with Draw Distance.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.15-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.15-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** with **Draw Distance** above Original, the labels on the profile and Controls screens went missing, and text in a race (such as the place, 3/3) could go dark or flicker. The game draws that text just in front of its button or backdrop, at a depth it works out for its own view distance, and a longer view distance had moved everything else by a hair. That text now moves with it, so it shows as it does at Original.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
