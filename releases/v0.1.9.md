San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.9: the Ghost Race car select draws correctly in a widescreen window.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.9-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.9-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** with Split-Screen Aspect Ratio at Expand, the Ghost Race car select (your car and the three ghost panels) was taken for a 4-player split screen in any window wider than 4:3: its words came apart ("NO G HOST AVAILA BLE") and the background picture was shifted. It now draws like the other menus, its text whole and the background mirrored out to the window's edges.

The [README](https://github.com/amazingalek/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
