San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.18: menu text in split screen stays inside its box.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.18-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.18-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** in two- to four-player split screen with **Split-Screen Aspect Ratio** at Expand, some menu text sat too far right, outside its box: the NO of the pause menu's quit question, the end of DEFAULTS on its Audio page, and the right-hand column of the scoreboard after a race or battle (DEATHS, BEST STUNT and their numbers). Text in a menu box now moves with the box when split screen fills a wide window.

The [README](https://github.com/amazingalek/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
