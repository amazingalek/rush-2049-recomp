San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.13: a longer draw distance and more detailed models.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.13-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.13-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**New:** two settings on the Graphics tab.

- **Draw Distance** (default 4x, also 16x): distant scenery is drawn from farther away, so less of it pops in, such as the building behind the walkway over the road on Track 1. Most of what the original leaves out is hidden behind something nearer anyway, so the difference shows only in places.
- **Level of Detail** (default High, also Highest): models keep their detailed versions farther out, so curves like the ramp before Track 1's tunnel stay round and small details such as traffic lights stay in view.

Original on both runs the game as it shipped. Together the defaults draw about 30% more a frame; on a weak GPU, Original on Draw Distance takes most of that back.

The [README](https://github.com/amazingalek/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
