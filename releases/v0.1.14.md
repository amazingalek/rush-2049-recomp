San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.14: far-off backdrops stay at their own distance.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.14-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.14-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Changed:** with **Draw Distance** above Original, the flat backdrops far off in a track (the boards painted with trees and buildings across the bay on Track 1) are placed at their own distance. The original game pulls any backdrop farther away than a set distance in to that distance, keeping it in the same place on screen, so it can cover scenery that is really in front of it and seems to keep its distance from the camera until it comes within reach. Original still does that, as the game shipped.

The [README](https://github.com/amazingalek/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
