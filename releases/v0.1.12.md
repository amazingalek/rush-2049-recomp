San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.12: menu letters no longer flicker to a wrong one.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.12-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.12-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** a letter of a menu's text, often in the menus at the end of a race, could flicker rapidly to a wrong-looking letter (the E of a player's name drawn like a ® sign). It happened when a picture the game had not drawn for a few frames came back while the graphics were a frame or two behind, so mostly where the graphics take a while to draw a frame, such as on a Steam Deck. The game no longer draws another picture in its place, in menus or in a race.

The [README](https://github.com/amazingalek/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
