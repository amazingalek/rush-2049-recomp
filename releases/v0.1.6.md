San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.6: the SAVE GHOST prompt shows the ghost already on each Controller Pak.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.6-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.6-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** with **Save Ghosts** on, the SAVE GHOST prompt said NO GHOST for every Controller Pak, even one already holding your ghost for that track, and saving replaced that ghost without asking. It now shows the ghost the pak holds, with its time, and asks OVERWRITE SAVED GHOST? before replacing it. The game's own check only worked for player names of exactly 6 letters; the ghosts themselves always saved and loaded correctly, on paks of either size.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
